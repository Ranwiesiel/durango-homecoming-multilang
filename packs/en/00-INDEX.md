## Notes from the source

- On the original site, click an animal row for per-part HP, part-break effects, and formulas
- HP/ATK/DEF assume **unstable factor = 1**
- **Unreachable** islands = no sailing route (colony `op`, city `ur`, event, trial) — data exists but not enterable
- Typical island herd estimate: **1 herd per ~650 land tiles**
- Synthetic elite islands: half-length terrain · 1 boss + 1 support herd · **no craters** (fork)
- Butcher counts are min–max ranges · parts may require gather skill ranks
